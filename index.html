Frequently asked questions

Get to know FlowStraw.

A quick introduction to the product concept and the problems it is designed to address.

What is FlowStraw?

FlowStraw is an AI-assisted data ingestion and preparation platform concept focused on helping teams turn raw tabular files into curated, modeled data for analytics.

What types of data can FlowStraw work with?

The current product positioning focuses on CSV, Excel, TSV and other structured tabular file formats. Specific source and destination support depends on the implementation.

How does AI fit into the workflow?

AI can assist with understanding incoming data, suggesting mappings, identifying potential relationships and accelerating transformation design. Human review and deterministic validation remain important.

Does FlowStraw create star schemas?

Star-schema modeling is a core part of the product vision: organizing data into fact and dimension structures that are easier to use in reporting and analytics.

Can FlowStraw support enterprise deployment requirements?

Deployment, security, governance and data-residency requirements should be evaluated as part of a proof of concept and implementation discussion.
